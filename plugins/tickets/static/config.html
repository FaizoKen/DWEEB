1 Menu topics

Each topic is one choice in the dropdown and opens a ticket tagged with it. Open a topic's Routing to send its tickets to their own category or team.

1 Who handles tickets

A category keeps tickets tidy. If it fills up (Discord allows 50 channels) or the bot can't use it, tickets open at the top level instead of failing.
