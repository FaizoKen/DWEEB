1 The question

DWEEB wires these onto your select menu as its options and locks them — nothing to map by hand.
2–25 options. The small box is an optional emoji shown beside the option.
Use in your message text: {question}{results}{votes}{leader}{status}{closes}
They stay live — every vote restamps them on the message. {results} draws the bars and counts.

2 Ballot rules

Ballots are always anonymous — nobody can see who voted for what.

3 When voting closes optional

Results reveal and voting stops at the deadline. A host can also close early or reopen — click the poll as a host, or right-click the message → Apps → Message Info → Manage poll.

Who can vote
